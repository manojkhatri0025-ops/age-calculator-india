Age calculation guide

How to Calculate Exact Age in Years, Months and Days

Learn how to calculate your exact age from your date of birth, including completed years, months and remaining days.

What does exact age mean?

Exact age is the amount of time between your date of birth and another selected date. Instead of showing only completed years, exact age can also show the remaining months and days.

For example, a person may be 25 years, 4 months and 12 days old. This gives more detail than simply saying that the person is 25 years old.

Why is exact age not calculated by dividing days by 365?

A simple calculation can divide the total number of days by 365, but this does not always give a calendar-accurate result. Different months have different numbers of days, and leap years add an extra day to February.

A calendar-based calculation counts complete years first, then complete months, and finally the remaining days. This makes the result easier to understand for birthdays and everyday planning.

How to calculate exact age

You can calculate exact age in three simple steps:

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Choose today or another calculation date.
  3. Read the result in years, months and days.

The calculation date is important. If you choose a future date, the result will show the age you will reach on that date. If the selected date is before the date of birth, the calculator should show a validation message.

How leap years affect age calculation

A leap year has 366 days instead of 365. It includes 29 February. When a date of birth falls on 29 February, the next birthday in a non-leap year needs a clear calendar rule.

A reliable age calculator should handle leap years and different month lengths rather than using only an average number of days.
