Privacy Policy

Read how Age Calculator India handles calculator inputs, share links, and contact messages.

What this website processes

Age Calculator India uses the information you enter into its calculator fields, such as a date of birth and a calculation date, to calculate a result in your browser.

Storage and transmission

Calculator results are not saved in a website database by the current code. The calculator is designed to process these inputs locally in the browser. A shareable result link may include the selected dates in the URL when you use the Copy link feature; do not share a link if you do not want those dates visible.

Contact messages

If you choose Send with Gmail, your email application opens a pre-filled Gmail compose window addressed to manojkhatri98570@gmail.com. Gmail then handles the message according to your Google account and Gmail settings.

Cookies and analytics

The current project does not intentionally add Google Analytics, AdSense, a database, or advertising scripts. The light/dark theme preference may be saved locally in your browser.
