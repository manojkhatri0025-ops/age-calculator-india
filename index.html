Useful to know

How the age calculator works

Age Calculator India compares your date of birth with a calculation date. It first counts complete calendar years, then complete months, and finally the remaining days. This gives a more useful answer than dividing total days by 365.

Why months and days can vary

Months have different lengths, and leap years add an extra day to February. The calculator uses calendar-aware date logic so your result stays meaningful around month ends and leap years.

India-ready date format

The interface is designed for Indian users with DD/MM/YYYY guidance, Indian English wording and Asia/Kolkata date handling. The calculator works directly in your browser.

Privacy first

Your date of birth is used only in the browser to calculate the result. It is not uploaded or stored by this website by default.
